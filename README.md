<h1 style="color: #d9a94b;">LH MP5</h1>

<p>Show an estimated five-second mana regeneration timer directly on the Blizzard player mana bar after casting.</p>

<p>A quiet visual reminder of WoW's five-second mana rule, placed right on the Blizzard player mana bar. It helps you see when a pause after casting is about to end without adding another frame to your screen.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">How it works</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Shows an estimated five-second timer after a successful spell with a reported mana cost.</li>
  <li>Ignores free spells and wand shots, and stays hidden while you use another resource.</li>
  <li>Clears the current timer when disabled and waits for the next qualifying spell when enabled again.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Settings</h2>

<p>Open <strong>Options → AddOns → LH Tweaks → LH MP5</strong> to turn the timer on or off. It is enabled by default and the choice is saved per character. The timer is an estimate, so treat it as a visual guide.</p>
